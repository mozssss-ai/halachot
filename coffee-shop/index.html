Our story

A tiny roaster in the back room.

We started with a 5 kg roaster and a stubborn idea: coffee is best when the people who roast it are the same people who pour it.

We buy directly from three farms we've visited, pay well above fair-trade minimums, and roast on Tuesdays so every bag you take home is fresh.
